## Contract notes (r11a)
- §20.2 vs §20.12: §20.12 says the seal target is the SHA-256 "of the UTF-8 body as stored (no JSON wrapping)"; §20.2, §20.11 and the prompt say the canonical JSON `{body_md, red_lines}`. Built as §20.2 (it binds the red lines); the server sends `body_sha256` and clients never recompute it, which §20.12 also says.
- Turn.kind gains `written` (a non-speaking company's written statement, by code); MeetingItem.speakers end with the devil's advocate (as the D2 fixtures).
- MeetingType gains `calendar` (`gregorian` | `jalali`: strategy's `FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=-1TH` is the last Thursday of the Jalali month) and `model_summary` (the standup's optional secretary summary); `members.devil` (`inspector` | `overseer`).
- Meeting gains `department` (a standup's) and `presence_note_fa` (audio until TTS); `GET /meetings` is a Page `{items, next_cursor}`.
- The reservation at the start is the meeting's cap (as the D2 fixtures show `reserved = cap`), not the estimate; with the 600K daily cap and the 0.15 share (90K) the board (120K) and strategy (150K) are `written` until the owner changes a budget, the cap or the share.
- A claim's source kinds the code can check: decision, resolution, deliverable, work, message, audit, knowledge, support_article (kb), incident, finding, code_session. Others (metric, revenue, kpi, okr, risk) are `unsourced` until a registry for them exists (r11b's KPI registry can add `kpi`).
- The employee layer in `GET /knowledge` has `ref: "MEM-…"`, kind `fact`, `valid_until: null` (memory has no expiry); `PUT /knowledge/KB-…` is 405 with code `validation_failed` (§14 has no 405 code).
- `knowledge.search` takes `layer` and `kind` (the prompt's filters; tools.md shows `{q, kinds?}` only).
- The minutes deliverable lives in the holding (producer HQ-SEC), whatever the meeting's company.
- A war room opened by an alert without an incident takes the incident that follows, else closes after 6 h. Postmortems are made for every incident closed after the deploy.
- `/internal/v1/charter/{scope}` (PUT) and `/versions/{v}/seal` (POST) exist only to refuse an agent (403) with an audit row.
- The authority-matrix row is read by OPA as `data.hq.authority.rules["knowledge.approve.company"].actor` (`owner` takes the CEOs' approval back), the shape r11b pushes (`{version, rules: {key: row}}`; seed row in `apps/api/seed/authority.yaml`).
- `governance.read` (A0; cards, OKR, risks, charter, outcomes) is r11b's (four of its five parts are §4-§8); its `charter` part reads `governance.charters` of this half (`hq_api.domain.charter.sealed()` is the same row).
