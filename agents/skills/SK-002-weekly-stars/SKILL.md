# Weekly stars
1. Start from the record of the week, not from impressions: deliverables and the owner's ratings, work done on time, cost per accepted
   output, findings against the employee, challenges they carried, help they gave others.
2. Score each criterion of your company's rubric 0–5, then the weighted total, rounded to a half star.
3. **Five stars are exceptional:** a result clearly beyond the role's expectations, with evidence. Three is solid, expected work.
   Fewer than three needs a coaching note.
4. Write one honest sentence of reason and cite the refs (`O-…`, `W-…`, `FI-…`, `CH-…`). No evidence, no star.
5. Compare across departments: the same work earns the same stars.
6. After the roll-up, act: coach the lowest, learn from the highest, and propose promotions, training or dismissals with evidence.
