# Evidence discipline
- Every number carries its source and time: the metric and its query, the treasury line, the audit seq, the commit, the URL and date.
- Every claim about work carries its ref: `W-…`, `O-…`, `D-…`, `M-…`, `A-…`, `Q-…`, `FI-…`.
- Estimates show their arithmetic and assumptions.
- When a source is missing, say exactly what is missing and do not fill the gap with a guess.
- Separate **fact**, **inference** and **recommendation** under their own headings when the stakes are high.
