# bitmart — the brand sheet (owner, 2026-11-26)

**The page:** @bitmart (IG-Hub account 25, company IG), the owner's fifth page: «قصه‌ی پول» — the real money
stories nobody told you, for everyone. **Independent:** it has nothing to do with the BitMart exchange — never
its logo, colours, token, app or referral (DIRECTION §0.2). The host is the owner, **بهنام جلالی**: his photo is
the page's mark.

## The two corporate colours (the owner's choice)
| | hex | where |

Support: Cream `#F5EFE6` (all text) · Navy panels `#162043` / raised `#1E2A55` · the deepest shadow `#090E20` ·
orange light `#FF8C5A` / shade `#D9521A` / glow `#FFB089` (second-level labels, roles, units) · muted `#9AA3BD` ·
documents: paper `#EFE6D3`, its shade `#D9CDB4`, ink `#17130E`. `tokens.json` holds them all.

## Three layers
1. **brand** — orange + navy (+ cream): visible in EVERY frame (the file bar at least).
2. **content** — plates and documents in their own colours, graded by `plate()` (navy shadows, warm light) or toned
   `duo` / `mono` (the archive look). No other dominant colour family: no mustard/gold field (DotCoin), no mint
   (Zarib100x), no violet/cyan (behnamjalali.tech).
3. **meaning** — up `▲ +` `#2FD67B`, down `▼ −` `#FF4766`, flat `■` muted; always the glyph and the sign. Orange is
   never a loss.

## Type (the owner's licensed fonts, fontiran 136710 — workshop only, never in git)
- **Anjoman Max** — hooks, headlines, numbers, kickers, the wordmark: SuperHeavy (990) / Black (900), ExBlack (950).
- **Ravagh** — captions (ExtraBold 800), labels and body (500–700).
- **IBM Plex Mono** (OFL, COMMON/fonts) — `FILE NNN` and Latin archive dates only.
Digits inside Persian are Persian; `$`-amounts and Latin names stay Latin, isolated; never both in one run.

## THE ORANGE MARKER (the signature)
A highlighter stroke with a hand-made edge, drawn right → left behind the ONE word that matters: in every hook
(`headline` with `[key]`), on the cover's title, on the end slate. One marker per screen; never on a whole line.
