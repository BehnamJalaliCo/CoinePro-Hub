## ONE FAMILY — HARD RULES (the owner, 2026-11-19; above everything below)
After P-000073 «BITCOIN ETF» (a navy ground: «it breaks the row») and P-000122 «SEED PHRASE» (the
legs cut by the pill) he ordered: every bitmart reel cover is ONE family, and a cover that breaks one
of these rules is never delivered.
1. **Always the orange ground.** A flat orange #FF6A2B background (as ChatGPT draws it), the GIANT
   ultra-bold condensed NAVY English words edge to edge at the TOP, behind him; him (his photo, a new
   suit every cover); the Persian title with its key word in a **rounded navy chip with orange
   letters**; the **navy pill** «کلمه‌ی «<word>» رو کامنت کن» (white letters, the word in orange);
   the **hand-drawn navy arrow** from the pill down toward the caption. Never a navy ground, never
   any other ground, never orange words on navy: the old A/B alternation is gone. (His two sample
   covers below are navy: they set the craft, NOT the ground.)
2. **The bottom is clean or whole.** Under the title, the pill and the arrow there is EITHER clean,
   flat orange (his body ends behind the title: waist up or knees up, nothing of him below the
   title) OR him full length down to his shoes. NEVER legs or a body cut by the pill or the arrow,
   never trousers showing under the pill.
3. **The row check before delivery.** `PY -I COMMON/tools/cover_sheet.py out/cover.jpg qc/cover_sheet.jpg`
   puts the new cover beside the page's last 4 covers (earlier sessions' qc/bitmart_cover.jpg, newest last; the first covers have fewer) and checks the ground. Read the sheet: the new cover must sit in that row — the
   same ground, the same navy words at the top, the same chip, pill and arrow, a clean or whole
   bottom, a suit and a pose that differ from the four beside it. If not, ask ChatGPT again naming
   exactly what is wrong (the ≤ 2 more tries of step 3 below).

## The fixed rules (his pack)
- **Two colours only for the design:** orange **#FF6A2B** and navy **#0F1630** (white only small).
  The cover is the brand layer of the page's three colour layers (brand/README.md); the owner
  approved this look on 2026-11-17. Inside the reel, the content is in full colour. On the cover,
  only the suit, the accessories and the element keep their natural colours.
  - **Always:** orange ground + navy English words + the Persian title in navy (one line may be
    white) with its key word in the navy chip (orange letters) + the navy pill. Every reel, both
    reels of a day, a pilot too. (Until 2026-11-19 the covers alternated A/B with a navy ground B;
    the owner stopped it: one family, rule 1.)
- **English backdrop:** ultra-bold condensed UPPERCASE (Anton or Bebas Neue), 1–2 lines, 2–12
  letters a line, behind the subject; one line may be drawn again as an outline in front of him
  (the reference's «PAGE»). Short and strong: `COLD WALLET`, `SEED PHRASE`, `HALVING`, `NEWS`, `DCA`.
- **Persian title:** Anjoman Max (SuperHeavy / Black; the owner's licensed font), 1–3 short lines, 72–110 px at 1080, ONE key word in a chip (or a number highlighted),
  set on the ground as in the samples (a solid bar only now and then, for variety). ChatGPT writes
  it (the owner's way, 2026-11-16); you check every letter, and set it yourself only as the last
  resort (step 4 below).
- **The call to action on the cover** when the reel has one (a comment word): the pill + the arrow
  of sample 2; otherwise the title alone.
- **Anatomy:** natural proportions — head : full body ≈ 1 : 7.2 (6.9–7.5), shoulders 2.6–3.0 head
  widths; never a big head; normal hands.
- **Identity:** his face exactly — bald head, eye spacing, eyebrows, nose, beard shape and density,
  jaw. Clothes and pose change every cover.
- **Clean:** no candles, coins, charts, neon, glow, particles, lens flares or 3D money by default;
  ONE support element that fits the video (below).
- **Logos:** a brand's real file, only when the reel is about it (top-left zone); never an AI logo.
- **Master 2160×3840, export 1080×1920.** Face, title and key element inside the critical safe zone
  (1080 units: x 60–1020, y 280–1650) and the grid's 3:4 crop (y 240–1680).
