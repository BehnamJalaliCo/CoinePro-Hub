# @behnamjalali.tech — the long YouTube videos' topic bank (owner 2026-11-18: one 8–15 minute video a day)

One topic per video, never twice. Seven lanes (README: the day's lane turns through them):
- **deep**: one current model or product end to end (what it is, how it works, prices, best uses, limits, how to start);
- **build**: a step-by-step build with AI (only what narrated motion graphics and code can show);
- **compare**: X vs Y with a clear verdict per use case;
- **explain**: how a technology really works;
- **story**: the inside story of a company, product or breakthrough, with its ending (what happened, where it is today);
- **career**: learning paths, skills, working with AI (never immigration or legal advice);
- **behind**: longer behind-the-scenes episodes of the AI holding (general terms only, as the reels' `behind`).
`topic` doubles as the working YouTube title (≤ 70 characters); `payoff` is the chapter outline (one point a
chapter). Rows of `deep` and `compare` describe the state of 2026-11-18: the session checks every fact and price
again on the official source the same day and says what changed.

## How the kit uses it (README step 1; only through `tools/topic.py`)
- `python3 -I KIT/tools/topic.py next --lane <lane>`; `find <words>`; `topic.claim` «bjtech-long: <topic>»; then
  `topic.py mark <#> --slot long --publish "۲۸ آبان"`. A lane used up: `topic.py add …`.
